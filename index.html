OUR PHILOSOPHY

How We Help Your Child Bloom

Applied Behavior Analysis (ABA) teaches essential skills by breaking them into achievable, bite-sized steps and celebrating every milestone. We keep every session warm, respectful, and led by your child's natural curiosity.

🎯

Plans Built With You

You know your child best. A Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA) listens to your family's priorities to create an individualized growth plan and reviews it with you continuously.

🧸

Learning Through Play

COMMON QUESTIONS

Questions Parents Ask

Finding the right support for your child is a big step. Here are answers to what families ask most.

Is ABA therapy right for my child? +
ABA therapy is evidence-based and effective for children with Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD), sensory integration challenges, and developmental delays. Our initial consultation gives you an honest, pressure-free evaluation of whether our approach matches your child's specific needs.
How many hours per week will my child need? +
Every child's program is personalized. Comprehensive programs may run 15–30 hours weekly, while focused programs (targeting specific skills like communication or social play) may require 8–12 hours. We always recommend schedules based on clinical necessity and your family's routine.
Can parents observe or participate in sessions? +
Absolutely! Parent participation is a cornerstone of our success. We encourage observation and host regular hands-on parent coaching sessions so you feel empowered using positive reinforcement strategies at home.
Do you accept health insurance? +
Yes, we work with many major insurance providers and assist families with benefit verification and pre-authorization. During your consultation, we will review your coverage options and guide you step-by-step.
