How we work with your child

ABA (applied behavior analysis) teaches skills by breaking them into small steps and rewarding progress. We keep sessions warm, play-based, and led by your child's interests.

Plans built with you

You tell us your goals. A Board Certified Behavior Analyst (BCBA) turns them into a plan and reviews it with you often.

Learning through play

Skills are taught inside games, snacks, and routines your child already enjoys, so progress carries over to home and school.

Progress you can see

We track data every session and share it in plain language, so you always know what is working.

Services

Programs are matched to your child's age, needs, and goals.

Early intervention

One-on-one therapy for young children building first words, play, and routines.

Communication skills

Speech, gestures, picture systems, or devices, whichever helps your child say what they need.

Social skills groups

Small groups that practice sharing, turn-taking, and making friends.

Daily living skills

Dressing, toileting, mealtimes, and other routines that build independence.

Parent training

Hands-on coaching so you can use the same strategies at home.

School transition support

Planning and coordination with teachers for a smooth move into school.

Questions parents ask

Is ABA therapy right for my child?

ABA can help children with autism and other developmental needs. The consultation and assessment help us tell you honestly whether our program fits.

How many hours a week will my child attend?

It depends on your child's goals and your family's schedule. We recommend a number after the assessment and adjust it as your child grows.

Can I watch or join sessions?

Yes. Parent involvement is part of the plan, and we will show you how to practice skills at home.

Do you accept insurance?

Ask us during your consultation. We will explain what is covered and the options available to you.
